## Brand & Style

This design system translates the precision, craft, and temperature transitions of specialty coffee roasting into a grounded, warm-editorial interface. It bridges direct-to-consumer discovery with high-density, analytical roastery management.

The visual style rejects glossy elevation, decorative gradients, floating drop shadows, and ornamental UI noise. Instead, it relies on an architectural, warm-minimalist foundation characterized by tonal structural planes, generous and unhurried whitespace, millimeter-precise 1px boundaries, and high-legibility typographic pairings. Every interface element feels purposeful, tactile, and engineered.

## Colors

The core palette reflects natural processing, parchment filters, caramelization, and kiln heat. Tonal planes and borders form visual separation without relying on artificial drop shadows.

### Core Interface Tokens

- **Background**: `#FAF6F1` (Light) / `#15100D` (Dark)
- **Cards & Surfaces**: `#FFFFFF` (Light) / `#211915` (Dark)
- **Secondary Surfaces**: `#F1E9DF` (Light) / `#2B211C` (Dark)
- **Structural Borders**: `#E4D9CC` (Light) / `#3A2E27` (Dark)
- **Text (Primary)**: `#1F1612` (Light) / `#F3ECE4` (Dark)
- **Text (Muted)**: `#6B5E55` (Light) / `#B8ABA0` (Dark)
- **Primary Action**: `#C2410C` (Light, text `#FFFFFF`) / `#F07B4F` (Dark, text `#1F1612`)
- **Accent**: `#D4A373` (Uniform warm caramel highlight)

### Data Visualization Specifications

The data visualization system splits into two distinct modes:

#### 1. Categorical / Multi-Category Palette

Designed for stacked bar graphs, distribution rings, and multi-series breakdowns (such as the _Order status mix by week_ chart). This palette is strictly categorical (non-sequential) and follows an invariant six-color sequence:

- **Light Mode Sequence**:
  1. Blue: `#2A78D6`
  2. Orange: `#EB6834`
  3. Emerald: `#1BAF7A`
  4. Amber: `#EDA100`
  5. Rose: `#E87BA4`
  6. Forest Green: `#008300`
- **Dark Mode Sequence**:
  1. Blue: `#3987E5`
  2. Orange: `#D95926`
  3. Emerald: `#199E70`
  4. Amber: `#C98500`
  5. Rose: `#D55181`
  6. Forest Green: `#008300`
- **Segment Spacing**: Stacked segments, touching bars, or slice boundaries must incorporate a clean 2px gap matching the host card background color (`#FFFFFF` in Light, `#211915` in Dark) to preserve legibility.

#### 2. Single-Series & Comparison Charts

Applied to linear time series (e.g., sales volume over time, volume by product category, and top products):

- **Primary Data Series**: Rendered in the accent color (`#C2410C` in Light, `#E4572E` in Dark) with solid geometry.
- **Previous Period Comparison**: Rendered in muted roast brown (`#6B5748` in Light, `#B8A394` in Dark) using a `2px` dashed stroke.
- **Gridlines**: `1px` dashed lines styled in border tones (`#E6DCD1` in Light, `#3A2C23` in Dark), rendered along the horizontal axis only. Vertical gridlines are suppressed.
- **Axis & Ticks**: Formatted in muted label typography at `12px` (`#6B5E55` in Light, `#B8ABA0` in Dark).

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional cadence between editorial character and tabular roasting analytics:

- **Display & Headings (Bricolage Grotesque)**: Imparts warmth, kinetic personality, and structural rhythm across page titles, modular card headers, and narrative sections.
- **Body & Controls (IBM Plex Sans / IBM Plex Sans Arabic)**: Applied to conversational copy, inputs, action labels, tooltips, and interface content to maintain crisp legibility.
- **Tabular Data & Metrics (IBM Plex Mono)**: Deployed for monetary values, timestamps, SKU tags, roast curves, batch identifiers, and chart axis scales.

### Numeric & Currency Formatting

Financial figures and unit measures are rendered strictly in `IBM Plex Mono` adhering to standard format (e.g. `EGP 420.00`). Chart tick labels use `label-data-sm` at `12px` to preserve clarity within dense analytical viewports.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through **Tonal Layering** and **Low-Contrast Outlines** rather than blur shadows:

- **Layered Surfaces**:
  1. Base canvas background (`#FAF6F1` / `#15100D`).
  2. Grouping containers, card recesses, and headers (`#F1E9DF` / `#2B211C`).
  3. Interactive foreground cards and chart canvases (`#FFFFFF` / `#211915`).
- **Crisp Structural Boundaries**: Every panel, table section, and input is bound by a `1px` solid border (`#E4D9CC` in Light, `#3A2E27` in Dark).
- **Interactive State Cues**: Depth on hover or focus is conveyed via a border shift to `#C2410C` (or `#D4A373`) and micro-shifts in background tone. Floating dropshadows are completely omitted.

## Shapes

The shape system leverages subtle, deliberate rounding that softens functional utility while retaining an architectural frame:

- **Interactive Inputs & Buttons**: Standard `0.5rem` (`8px`) to `0.75rem` (`12px`) corner radius.
- **Cards & Data Modules**: `1rem` (`16px`) to `1.25rem` (`20px`) corner radius.
- **Status Pills & Chips**: Pill-shaped (`9999px` full capsule) to clearly distinguish metadata tags from structural buttons.

## Components

### Data Visualization Panels & Charts

- **Multi-Category & Stacked Charts**:
  - Uses the six designated categorical colors in strict order (`#2A78D6`, `#EB6834`, `#1BAF7A`, `#EDA100`, `#E87BA4`, `#008300` in Light; `#3987E5`, `#D95926`, `#199E70`, `#C98500`, `#D55181`, `#008300` in Dark).
  - Touching segments (such as bars in the _Order status mix by week_ chart) must feature a `2px` separation mask matching the card background (`#FFFFFF` Light / `#211915` Dark).
- **Single-Series Trend Lines & Bars**:
  - Primary metric line/bar: Accent tone (`#C2410C` Light / `#E4572E` Dark).
  - Secondary / Previous period comparison: `2px` dashed stroke in muted roast brown (`#6B5748` Light / `#B8A394` Dark).
  - Grid: `1px` dashed lines in border color (`#E6DCD1` Light / `#3A2C23` Dark), horizontal only.
  - Axes: `IBM Plex Mono` at `12px` with muted color tones (`#6B5E55` Light / `#B8ABA0` Dark).

### Buttons

- **Primary**: Background `#C2410C` (Dark: `#F07B4F`), text `#FFFFFF` (Dark: `#1F1612`), radius `12px`, min height `44px`, font-weight `600`.
- **Secondary (Outlined)**: 1px border `#E4D9CC` (Dark: `#3A2E27`), transparent fill, text `#1F1612` (Dark: `#F3ECE4`).
- **Ghost**: Transparent fill, text `#6B5E55`, active hover `#F1E9DF`.

### Cards & Modules

- Clean rectangular containers with `16px`–`20px` corner radius, bounded by `1px` solid border (`#E4D9CC` Light / `#3A2E27` Dark).
- Padding: `24px` (`space-lg`) on desktop, `16px` (`space-md`) on mobile.

### Inputs & Form Controls

- Height: `44px` minimum with `1rem` horizontal padding.
- Border: `1px` solid `#E4D9CC` (Dark: `#3A2E27`). Focus state transitions strictly to `#C2410C` without outer glow rings.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls

- Size: `20px x 20px`, `1px` solid border. Checkboxes use `4px` corner radius; radios are circular. Active selection fills `#C2410C`.

### Badges & Category Chips

- Rendered as `9999px` full capsule pills using `IBM Plex Mono` or `IBM Plex Sans` at `12px`.
- Inactive or category chips use secondary surface backing (`#F1E9DF` Light / `#2B211C` Dark) with a `1px` border.
